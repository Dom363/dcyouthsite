Got Questions?

FAQ

The most common questions, answered. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out below.

Who can come to DC Youth?
DC Youth is for high schoolers, years 7 to 12. If you're high-school-aged but not currently at school (homeschooled, doing a trade, between schools), you're still welcome. Just come along.
What happens on a typical Friday night?
Doors open at 6pm with food and time to hang. Around 6:30pm we all come together to kick the night off, then move into worship. After that it's either a message, small groups, or games depending on the night. Wraps up at 8:30pm.
How much does it cost?
Entry is free. Free food every week. We also have a café that sells lollies, soft drinks and a few other snacks for purchase if you want extras.
What should I bring?
Yourself. That's it. A few dollars to grab something from the café, and a Bible and notebook to take notes on the message.
What should I wear?
Whatever you'd wear to hang out with friends. Comfortable and modest is the rule of thumb. Heads up that some nights involve running around or messy games, so don't show up in something you'd be devastated to ruin.
What does drop-off and pickup look like?
Drop-off from 6pm at the front entrance of the church, 3 Chisholm Rd, Carrara. Sign in closes at 6:30pm, so don't be late. Night ends at 8:30pm. Pickup by 9pm at the latest.
Can my child get a lift to or from youth?
Yes. We organise lifts through our leaders. If you genuinely can't get a safe lift any other way, reach out through the contact form below or DM us on Instagram and we'll sort it. If you do have another option, please use that so we can focus on the young people who don't.
What if I'm not religious or not sure about church?
You're welcome here. Seriously. You don't need to believe anything or know anything to walk through the door. Plenty of people come along curious, sceptical, or just for the friends. All we ask is that you give the worship and message moments their space and stay respectful of what's happening in the room.
Does youth still run if the weather's bad?
Yes. We're indoors, so rain or shine we're going ahead. The only time we'd cancel is for a major weather event, and we'd let everyone know on our Instagram if that happens.
Do you run during school holidays?
DC Youth doesn't run during the school holidays, but we do often put on special events. Keep an eye on the Events section above, or follow @dcyouth__ on Instagram so you don't miss anything.
How do I stay updated on what's happening?
Check the events on this site for what's coming up, and follow @dcyouth__ on Instagram for everything else.
